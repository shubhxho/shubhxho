---
title: Small tools, complete thoughts
description: Why I keep shipping focused software instead of waiting for a grand system.
date: 2026-08-18
---

Most useful software starts with a narrow question. Can a PDF carry a game? Can a simulator make a robotics idea easier to test? Can a command line tool remove one repetitive step?

I like projects with a clear edge. Their constraints make decisions easier, and a finished tool teaches more than an ambitious draft. The point is not to make something small for its own sake. It is to make a complete thought that someone can run, inspect, and extend.

## The loop

Build the smallest version that proves the idea. Put it in front of people. Watch where it breaks. Then keep the part that still feels alive.

That loop works for systems work, AI experiments, and physical machines alike. A good tool earns its next feature.
