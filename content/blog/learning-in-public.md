---
title: Learning where the work is
description: A short note on choosing work that creates momentum.
date: 2026-08-10
---

The fastest way I know to learn is to work near an honest problem. It gives study a direction and makes feedback immediate.

That is why I move between code, robotics, research, and communities. Each one exposes a different kind of constraint: latency, physics, clarity, or people. The overlap is where interesting work tends to appear.

## A practical rule

Choose projects that leave a trace. A repository, a demo, a write-up, or a person who can use what you made is better than a private idea that never meets the world.
