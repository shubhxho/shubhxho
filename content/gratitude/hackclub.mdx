Volunteering at [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) showed me that teenagers with internet access can outship most institutions — and that **community is a multiplier**.

## What stuck

- Ship nights where the default answer is "did you push it?"
- A network of builders who are reachable before they are famous
- Proof that place does not cap ambition

I organized the [first hackathon in Khagaria](/history) with their ecosystem. That would not have happened without people who treat high schoolers like founders.
