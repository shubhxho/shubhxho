Shipped [kinetic](https://github.com/shubhxho/kinetic), a native macOS robotics simulator. I wanted to drop a mechanism in a world and watch it fail before I spend money on motors.

Hardware from Khagaria is slow to replace. A simulator that lies a little is still cheaper than a part that arrives wrong.
