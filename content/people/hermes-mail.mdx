[Hermes](https://github.com/hackclub/hermes) wraps [Theseus](https://mail.hackclub.com) so Hack Club events can actually send mail — letters, packets, postage math, Slack when something ships.

It is unglamorous infrastructure: queues, costs, notifications. That is the kind of system that makes a community feel real instead of online-only.

I look up to the people who keep [mail.hackclub.com](https://mail.hackclub.com) moving. Same ecosystem as [Hack Club](/people/hackclub).
